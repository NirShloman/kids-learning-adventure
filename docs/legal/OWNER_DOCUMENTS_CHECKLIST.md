# Documents the owner must obtain

Do not mark an asset approved merely because a box below is checked; retain the signed/source document and record its path and SHA-256 in the asset register.

## Music and recordings

- [ ] Identity and contact details of composer, lyricist (if any), arranger, performer(s), producer, and sound-recording/master owner for each MP3.
- [ ] Signed assignment or commercial license for both the musical composition and the sound recording.
- [ ] Rights expressly cover synchronization with games/video, reproduction, adaptation/editing/looping, worldwide app-store and web distribution, marketing captures, duration, sublicensing to Apple/Google/CDN providers, and attribution.
- [ ] Invoice/receipt, source DAW session, stems, original masters, ISRC or other creation records.
- [ ] Confirmation that no uncleared samples, performances, voices, or third-party compositions are included.

## AI images, characters, props, and video

- [ ] Exact service/product, model/version, account holder, account type/plan, generation date, and generation ID.
- [ ] Full prompts, negative prompts, seed/settings where available, input/reference files, and input-rights evidence.
- [ ] Download/export receipt and untouched original output; record hashes before editing.
- [ ] Snapshot/PDF of terms and service-specific rules effective on the actual generation date and territory.
- [ ] Editing/project files and a dated description of meaningful human selection, composition, drawing, animation, or retouching.
- [ ] Similarity/search review for distinctive third-party characters, brands, artwork, and persons.
- [ ] Recover `uploads/pasted-1785302721105-0.png` referenced by the Claude props README.

## Nir, Shir, and human likeness

- [ ] Signed owner declaration answering whether either character is based on, resembles, or was generated from a real person.
- [ ] Adult model release where applicable.
- [ ] Guardian consent/release and age-at-creation record if any reference depicts a minor.
- [ ] Photographer/artist rights for every reference image; a model release does not replace copyright permission.
- [ ] Permission for commercial app distribution, animation, modification, advertising, and worldwide/long-term use.

## Code, text, educational content, and contractors

- [ ] Owner authorship declaration identifying the actual legal owner and all contributors.
- [ ] Written employee/contractor IP assignment, confidentiality terms, and moral-right/credit treatment where legally permitted.
- [ ] Source list for educational facts and confirmation that protected wording, illustrations, question sets, or compositions were not copied.
- [ ] Business/developer legal name, postal address, support email, privacy contact, and store seller identity.
- [ ] Lawyer review of the final rights file, privacy policy, terms, consumer disclosures, and brand clearance.
