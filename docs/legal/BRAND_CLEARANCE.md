Search date: 2026-08-22. This is a preliminary exact-term screen, not legal clearance or a similarity/opposition analysis.

## Owner-selected name — ידע׳לה / Yedale

Owner spelling confirmation: 2026-08-27.

- The 2026-08-26 Israeli trademark query returned no exact `YEDALE` or `ידעלה` result. That query covered the earlier unpunctuated Hebrew spelling and does not document an exact or similarity search for `ידע׳לה`.
- Apple App Store queries in the Israeli and US storefronts did not return an exact app-title match; a Google Play exact-name screen found no match.
- Public web searches returned personal-name references, not a child-learning or software brand using the exact name.
- `yedale.com` and `yedale.app` did not resolve and returned “not found” from their respective registry RDAP endpoints on the review date. Availability can change at any moment and is not proof of legal availability.
- The owner-selected public spelling is `ידע׳לה` in Hebrew and `Yedale` in Latin script, pronounced `יֶדַעְלֶה`.

Result: `REVIEW REQUIRED`. The Latin-name screen and prior Hebrew search remain useful background, but the corrected Hebrew spelling requires a refreshed exact, punctuation-normalized, phonetic, transliteration, and similarity search. Before public release, obtain legal advice for the intended goods and territories, register the core domains and social handles, and file the approved Hebrew and Latin word marks in the relevant classes. Do not use the ® symbol until registration is granted.

## Current names

### Noni / נוני

Review date: 2026-08-26.

- An exact-name iPhone app, `noni - AI Assistant`, is currently listed in the Apple App Store.
- `Noni for Teachers` is currently listed in the Apple App Store in Education, is rated 4+, and is published by Teaching Strategies. Teaching Strategies also uses `noni™` for early-childhood educational resources.
- `noni.kids` is an active child-facing educational platform for interactive narrated stories, language development, families, and schools.
- `Pep & Noni` is an interactive children's story/game product for ages 2-6, and ABC Cool Kids uses a character named Noni in preschool learning and phonics content.
- The exact domains `noni.com`, `noni.app`, `noni.kids`, and `noni.co.il` all resolved during the review and should be treated as unavailable unless independently acquired.
- US trademark records and marketplace evidence include `NONI` filings/use for downloadable educational software, child development, teaching materials, and early-childhood education. Current status, ownership, territorial coverage, and similarity must be confirmed by trademark counsel in every intended market.
- No reliable conclusion about Israeli registration can be drawn from indexed search results alone. A full Israeli search must cover exact, contains, phonetic, transliterated, visually similar, and related-goods results in classes 9 and 41 (and class 42 if SaaS is offered).

Result: `HIGH / DO NOT ADOPT` as the standalone app or master-brand name. The exact app-store collision, same-field educational use, child-product use, weak search ownership, and domain unavailability make the name commercially unsuitable even before a final legal opinion. Do not use `Noni`, `נוני`, `Nony`, or a compound dominated by that term without written trademark-counsel clearance.

The family story may be retained privately as the inspiration for the brand project, without presenting Noni as the public product or character name.

### Kids Learning Adventure

- Exact-title collision found: an existing US App Store listing named [Kids Learning Adventure](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kids-learning-adventure/id6745814193), published in 2025 in Education and made for young children.
- Internet results also show education/child-care uses of “Kids Learning Adventure” and a historical US filing for “KLA KIDS LEARNING ADVENTURE.” Even where a filing is abandoned, marketplace use and confusing similarity still require counsel review.
- Result: `HIGH` risk as a store-facing primary name; it is also highly descriptive and likely weak as a distinctive mark.

### לומדים בכיף

- The exact phrase produced no record in the downloadable Israeli trademark dataset queried on 2026-08-22; a broader search for “לומדים” produced 11 records, mostly closed, plus unrelated pending marks.
- Web results show widespread descriptive use in educational material and services. Absence of an exact database hit does not establish availability or registrability.
- Result: `MEDIUM/HIGH` distinctiveness risk. Obtain phonetic, visual, translation, owner, and common-law/use searches.

Likely Nice classes for counsel to assess: class 9 (downloadable software/mobile apps), class 41 (education and entertainment), and class 42 only if an online/SaaS service is actually offered. Goods/services wording matters more than class numbers alone.
